THE LONG WALK

A story told in
six steps.

From an evolutionary success story to an engineering challenge, the hexapod never stopped moving.

01

Nature’s blueprint

Six-legged arthropods helped shape ecosystems for hundreds of millions of years. Their segmented bodies, compact scale, and alternating gaits made an adaptable template.

02

Machines begin to walk

Robotics researchers looked to insects when wheeled systems met uneven terrain. Six legs offered stability without the complexity of balancing a biped.

03

The next step

Today, sensors and autonomy are making walking machines more aware, while nature keeps offering new lessons to observe and understand.

Mantis

Matt Denton / Micromagic Systems

Purpose
Rideable walking machine and large-scale robotics demonstration.

Approximately 2.8 m tall and nearly two tonnes; cockpit joystick or remote Wi-Fi control; 18 hydraulic actuators.

Technology
Articulated legs with hook-based gripping feet and coordinated climbing control.

21Research robot

Capabilities
Robots communicate and coordinate actions to solve shared tasks.
Technology

22Research robot

AntBot

Julien Dupeyroux, Julien Serres and Stéphane Viollet / Aix-Marseille University and CNRS

Purpose
Outdoor navigation inspired by desert ants.
Capabilities
GPS-free homing; reported return error about 5 cm after almost 14 m of travel; 18 leg actuators.
Technology
3D-printed structure, polarized-skylight compass, 12-pixel optic-flow sensor and step counting.
Photos / demoProject source

18 elastic joints; adaptive walking and obstacle-climbing reflexes.
Technology
Lightweight exoskeleton, custom elastic joint drives, multiple sensors and Walknet decentralized control.

24Research robot

LAURON (V / VI)

Autonomous inspection and locomotion in challenging terrain.
Capabilities
Insect-inspired terrain negotiation; V adds leg articulation; VI supports dynamic walking research.

25Research robot

ATHLETE

NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory

Purpose
Prototype cargo transport and manipulation for lunar exploration.
Capabilities
Six wheel-ended limbs; rolls or walks; first-generation prototype rated for 300 kg payload in Earth gravity.
Technology
Six degrees of freedom per limb plus wheel actuation, stereo vision and interchangeable manipulation tools.
Photos / demoProject source

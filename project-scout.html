Scout

An open-source walker built to learn the essentials of stable motion on difficult ground.

18 servos 6 legs 1 shared codebase

Where curiosity
gets traction.

Scout is deliberately approachable: standard hobby servos, a simple frame, and an Arduino brain. Its focus is the fundamental tripod gait—the rhythm that makes six legs feel purposeful.
